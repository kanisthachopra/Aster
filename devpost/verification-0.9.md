# Coaching revision 0.9 verification

10 October 2026. Focus: a useful review before further world polish.

## What changed

- Sustained movement checks replace isolated angle narration. A marked moment has an observation, a practical cue, a purpose and a bounded replay.
- Complete local pull-up reps can be checked within a multi-rep set; clipped endpoints, interruptions and tracking spikes abstain.
- A short optional goal, variation and comfort check-in changes the review. Pain or recurrent instability supersedes generic cues; no fixed rep count, forced shoulder position or muscle-activation claim is inferred.
- Animated phone/framing instructions cover all three exercises, landscape/portrait, both ends of the movement, play/pause and reduced motion. This is a capture illustration, not a professional technique reference.
- Local follow-up questions are spoken on demand. Opted-in Nebius explanations select existing reviewed text only; strict server schema, trusted cards, health-question bypass and fallback prevent invented findings.
- Saved journals receive actionable coaching notes rather than a collection of angle facts. Existing account, activity and credit rules are preserved.

## What the 600 recordings establish

The reproducible replay inspected all 600 actual cached pose recordings, containing 22,536 frames. It performed no new inference or network requests, and left the source manifest unchanged. The new checks produced specific observations on 128 recordings: 127 received narrow strengths and seven received corrections (overlap exists). All corrections were push-up hip-position checks. The remaining 472 received general practice guidance. No pull-up or squat corrections fired in this archive.

This is an honest coverage result, not correction precision, human agreement, technique safety or proof that a declined clip has good form. The action labels cannot validate those outcomes. The new pull-up segmentation passes independent mechanical tests but does not improve these archive counts. Squat coordination still checks opposite-direction motion, not every case of hips rising faster than the chest.

The qualified-reviewer annotation protocol requires fresh source-group splits, independent cue/evidence labels and reporting both correctness and coverage. No reinforcement learning or 80% form-confidence result is claimed. See [research](coaching-research-0.9.md), [protocol](../evaluation/coaching-validation/protocol.md) and [aggregate replay](../evaluation/coaching-validation/coverage-0.9.json).
