# Scanned surface provenance

Downloaded 2026-10-08 from the official Poly Haven API/CDN. All six maps are 2K JPG: diffuse, OpenGL normal and packed ambient-occlusion/roughness/metallic.

- [Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry), photographed by Dimitrios Savva and processed by Rico Cilliers, CC0.
- [Aerial Rocks 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_rocks_02), Rob Tuytel, CC0.
- [Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license).

`provenance.json` records the exact download URLs and source sizes. Files are served locally; playing the app does not send requests to Poly Haven. In-app tinting gives the terrestrial scans a dry extraterrestrial palette; they are artistic terrain assets, not a scientific Mars reconstruction.

The original procedural environment probe and habitat-panel texture are generated by SurfaceMaterials.ts.
